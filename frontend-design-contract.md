# Frontend Design Contract

## screenArchitecture
folding-instrument

## aboveTheFold
A horizontal aroma fan occupies the working surface. Its hinged leaves represent frozen sensory families. The steep ledger is a narrow vertical strip, while the writable tasting slip is attached to the active leaf.

## primaryInteraction
The user loads a flight, opens an unfilled family leaf, writes a descriptor plus sensory grounding, and pins it on-chain.

## layoutMap
Slim steep ledger at left, folding aroma fan across the center, tasting slip at lower right, and a stitched transaction seam along the bottom.

## visualMetaphor
A shared cupping notebook built as a hinged paper aroma fan.

## motionSystem
Steam Curl drifts above the cup. Leaf Unfold opens the selected family. Ink Soak reveals accepted notes. Palate Wash desaturates after rejection. Binding Settle pulses after chain refresh.

## effectStack
CSS steam ribbons and perspective accordion folds.

## componentShapeLanguage
Hinged paper leaves, ink stamps, cup rings, stitched ledger edges, and narrow tasting slips.

## customVisualComponents
AromaFan, SteamCup, SteepLedger, TastingSlip, FatigueWash, and TransactionSeam.

## bannedFromThisBuild
No generic centered hero, no equal feature cards, no horizontal stats strip, no chip row, no submit-form-to-feed skeleton, no three-column footer, no dark navy plus purple gradient, no radial compass, and no gallery plinths.

## proofOfDifference
Archive Lantern is a dark spatial gallery with a roaming spotlight and labels. Tea Compass is a light horizontal paper instrument whose interaction is choosing and unfolding a sensory family. Its result is a completed fan, not a room reveal.
